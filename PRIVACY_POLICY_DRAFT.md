Ligaya ("the app") is an emergency-response app. This policy explains what information it
collects, why, and how you can control it.

## Information we collect

**Location.** When you start an emergency (by voice or the SOS button), Ligaya uses your
device's precise or approximate location to share with your emergency contacts and, where
available, with emergency dispatch. Location is not collected in the background outside of an
active emergency.

**Voice and speech.** Ligaya's voice-activated SOS and Emergency Companion features listen for a
wake phrase and process your speech to understand emergency requests and hold a conversation
during an emergency. Audio is transcribed on your device; the resulting text (not raw audio) is
sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. We do not store raw audio recordings.

**Emergency contacts and household/family information.** Names, phone numbers, and relationship
information for the people you add to your Safety Circle, so Ligaya can alert them during an
emergency.

**Emergency and notification history.** A record of emergencies you've triggered and the delivery
status of alerts sent to your contacts, so you and your household can see what happened during
past emergencies.

**Account information.** [Once account creation is wired in:] an email address and profile
information you provide when creating an account.

**Purchase information.** [Once billing is wired in:] whether you have an active Ligaya+
subscription. Payment details themselves (card numbers, etc.) are handled entirely by Google Play
Billing — Ligaya never receives or stores them.

## How we use this information

Solely to provide the app's core safety features: locating you during an emergency, alerting your
chosen contacts, connecting you to emergency dispatch, powering the voice assistant, and
maintaining your emergency history and Safety Circle. We do not sell your data, and we do not use
it for advertising.

## How we share this information

- With the emergency contacts you configure, when you trigger an emergency.
- With emergency dispatch services, when you place an emergency call.
- With Google, as the processor of: Gemini (for voice-request understanding and conversational
  responses), [Places API, for nearby-help lookups, once enabled], and Google Play Billing (for
  subscription purchases).
- We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Your controls

You can review and remove your Safety Circle contacts at any time in the app. You can request
deletion of your account and all associated data by [contact method — email/support link]. Denying
location or microphone permission disables the corresponding feature but does not prevent you from
using the rest of the app; the SOS button itself does not require either permission.
